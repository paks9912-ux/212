## Профиль

**Title**
> Ordering Websites for Restaurants — Menu, Cart, Orders to WhatsApp

**Hourly rate:** начать с $15/hr. Поднимать после первых 3 отзывов.

**Overview**

```
I build ordering websites for restaurants, cafes and food delivery.

Your guest picks dishes, fills in the address, and you receive the
complete order in WhatsApp or Telegram as a single message: items,
quantities, address, payment method, total. Nobody retypes anything,
nobody recalculates totals by hand.

What the site does:
• Menu by category, cart, quantity controls
• Delivery and pickup — pickup automatically drops the delivery fee
• Minimum order amount, free-delivery threshold with progress bar
• Payment method selection, order notes
• Field validation — an order cannot be sent without phone and address
• Cart survives a page reload
• Dark and light theme, works on any phone
• QR menu for tables in the dining room

Why clients pick this over the usual options:
• No commission on orders — money goes straight to you, not a platform
• No server to maintain — the site is a single static file, so there is
  nothing to crash and no monthly hosting bill
• You own the source. It keeps working without me.
• Launch in 24 hours, not two weeks

Send your menu in any form — a photo, a spreadsheet, a screenshot, or
just a list in a message. I will build it and show you the result
before you approve anything.
```

**Skills:** HTML5, CSS, JavaScript, Web Design, Landing Page, Restaurant,
WhatsApp, Responsive Design, QR Code, Web Development
